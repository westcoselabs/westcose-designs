# WestCose Designs — Project Scope & Company Breakdown

**Document Type:** Project Foundation / Scope  
**Project:** WestCose Designs Website Redesign  
**Primary Domain:** `westcosedesigns.com`  
**Status:** Working Direction — Approved Foundation  
**Purpose:** Define what WestCose Designs is as a company, what the redesigned website must accomplish, what belongs within the project, and how WestCose Designs relates to the broader WestCose ecosystem.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

WestCose Designs is an independent multidisciplinary design studio focused on creating bold, memorable visual identities and design systems for organizations, brands, institutions, and apparel-driven businesses.

The studio combines two sides of design that are often separated:

- **Expressive creative work** — illustration, mascots, apparel graphics, streetwear, custom artwork, and highly visual brand concepts.
- **Structured professional identity work** — brand systems, corporate identity, stationery, institutional branding, guidelines, collateral, and scalable visual systems.

The redesigned `westcosedesigns.com` website will position WestCose Designs as a **design-focused portfolio and client acquisition platform**.

Its primary job is to:

1. Showcase the quality and range of WestCose design work.
2. Establish credibility with serious commercial, institutional, and corporate clients.
3. Communicate WestCose's personality and creative point of view.
4. Rank organically for relevant design services and project categories.
5. Generate qualified design inquiries.
6. Connect visitors to the broader WestCose ecosystem without diluting the focus of the site.

The website will not function as the primary software portfolio or ecommerce platform.

Those areas belong to separate WestCose properties:

- **WestCose Designs** — branding, identity, illustration, apparel design, and visual systems.
- **WestCose Labs** — websites, software, SaaS, experiments, and digital products.
- **WestCose Shop** — WestCose streetwear, merchandise, apparel, and physical products.

# 2. Company Definition

## WestCose Designs

WestCose Designs is a design studio built around the belief that strong visual work should be recognizable, useful, and difficult to ignore.

The studio does not exist solely to create isolated logos.

WestCose develops visual systems that can extend across:

- brand identity
- print
- digital applications
- apparel
- merchandise
- signage
- stationery
- campaigns
- illustration
- environmental graphics
- production assets
- brand standards

The studio's value comes from being able to move between **high-concept creative work and practical production systems**.

WestCose should feel capable of designing:

- a school district identity system,
- a healthcare stationery suite,
- a bold mascot illustration,
- a streetwear collection,
- a corporate logo system,
- an apparel graphic,
- or an entire family of branded applications,

without losing consistency or personality.

# 3. Positioning

## Core Position

WestCose Designs should be positioned as a:

> **Multidisciplinary independent design studio creating brand identities, illustration, apparel graphics, and visual systems for organizations that do not want to look ordinary.**

The studio should not present itself as a generic freelance graphic design service.

The brand should feel closer to a small creative studio or independent design practice capable of handling both focused assignments and larger identity systems.

---

## Positioning Attributes

WestCose Designs should consistently communicate the following qualities:

### Bold

The work should have presence and visual confidence.

### Crafted

Projects should feel deliberately designed rather than assembled from templates.

### Versatile

The portfolio should demonstrate the ability to work across industries, audiences, and applications.

### System-Oriented

WestCose should show that good identity work extends beyond a logo into a usable visual system.

### Illustrative

Custom illustration is a major differentiator and should remain central to the brand.

### Production-Aware

Designs are created with real-world applications in mind, including print, signage, embroidery, screen printing, digital assets, and vendor handoff.

### Independent

WestCose should maintain the personality, experimentation, and flexibility of an independent studio rather than attempting to imitate a large corporate agency.

### Memorable

The studio's design philosophy should consistently favor distinctiveness over generic visual trends.

# 4. WestCose Personality

The new website should preserve a strong WestCose point of view.

WestCose is not sterile corporate minimalism.

It combines:

- premium design presentation
- gritty streetwear influences
- illustration culture
- California / Bakersfield roots
- blue-collar visual references
- print and production details
- bold typography
- restrained humor
- experimentation
- modern digital presentation

The brand can be serious without becoming boring.

It can be expressive without becoming chaotic.

The goal is to create a studio presence that feels **high-end, capable, independent, and unmistakably WestCose**.

# 6. Primary Business Objectives

The redesigned WestCose Designs website must serve the business before it serves experimentation.

The cinematic presentation and advanced interactions are intended to support these objectives rather than replace them.

## Objective 1 — Generate Qualified Leads

The website should make it easy for prospective clients to understand:

- what WestCose does,
- what type of projects are a good fit,
- the level of work WestCose is capable of,
- and how to begin a project.

The primary call to action should center around **starting a project**.

---

## Objective 2 — Increase Perceived Value

The current body of work should be presented in a way that communicates a higher level of strategy, craft, and capability.

Projects should not appear as disconnected images.

Strong projects should be framed as systems, case studies, or intentional bodies of work.

---

## Objective 3 — Demonstrate Range Without Losing Focus

The portfolio should show that WestCose can operate across:

- institutional branding,
- corporate identity,
- healthcare,
- education,
- apparel,
- illustration,
- mascots,
- local business,
- merchandise,
- and brand development.

However, these categories must still feel like they come from one studio.

---

## Objective 4 — Improve Organic Search Visibility

The website should preserve existing SEO value while expanding into a clearer hierarchy of dedicated service pages and project pages.

The redesign should improve:

- content structure,
- internal linking,
- semantic page hierarchy,
- service-specific landing pages,
- case-study depth,
- metadata,
- structured data,
- image optimization,
- page speed,
- accessibility,
- and topical relevance.

Existing SEO authority associated with `westcosedesigns.com` should be protected during migration.

---

## Objective 5 — Create a Memorable Brand Experience

The site should be visually impressive enough that the experience itself becomes evidence of WestCose's creative capabilities.

The website should feel like a designed experience rather than a portfolio template.

Motion, shaders, scroll choreography, page transitions, and interactive details should reinforce the studio's creative identity.

# 13. Domain Strategy

## Primary Domain

`westcosedesigns.com`

This should remain the primary domain for the design studio during this redesign.

Reasons:

- existing domain history
- existing search authority
- existing indexed pages
- backlinks
- established relevance
- reduced migration risk

A platform migration from WordPress to Next.js should not be combined unnecessarily with a full domain migration.

---

## WestCose.com

`westcose.com` should be treated as a valuable umbrella-brand asset.

Potential long-term uses include:

- WestCose ecosystem gateway
- parent brand landing page
- redirect to WestCose Designs
- central navigation between Designs, Labs, and Shop

Its final purpose can be decided separately without risking the SEO equity of `westcosedesigns.com`.

# 17. SEO Content Strategy

The redesigned site should increase search visibility by creating a clear relationship between:

**Service → Supporting Work → Case Study → Related Service**

Example:

```text
/services/brand-identity/
        ↓
/work/greenfield-union-school-district/
        ↓
/services/logo-design/
/services/corporate-collateral/
```

Each major service should have enough unique content to rank independently while project pages provide supporting evidence and topical depth.

# 18. Design Philosophy for the Website

The website itself should demonstrate WestCose's design philosophy.

It should be:

- visually bold
- highly intentional
- cinematic
- interactive
- modern
- technically advanced
- content-driven
- responsive
- accessible
- performant
- SEO-friendly

Advanced motion should never prevent:

- navigation,
- content discovery,
- keyboard use,
- mobile access,
- reduced-motion support,
- search engine indexing,
- or fast loading.

The experience should be impressive because the interaction is thoughtful, not because every element moves.

# 19. What WestCose Designs Is Not

WestCose Designs should not be positioned as:

- a generic full-service marketing agency
- a template-based logo shop
- a web development company
- a software consultancy
- an ecommerce store
- a low-cost production design service
- an AI-generated design service

These distinctions help maintain a clear identity.

Website and software work should move toward WestCose Labs.

Physical WestCose products should move toward the Shop.

WestCose Designs should remain focused on visual design.

# 20. Competitive Advantage

WestCose's strongest advantage is the combination of several capabilities rarely presented together.

### Illustration + Identity Systems

The studio can create both original expressive artwork and organized professional identity systems.

### Streetwear + Corporate Design

WestCose understands bold apparel graphics while also producing credible institutional and corporate materials.

### Creative Direction + Production

Projects can move from visual concept through practical applications and final vendor-ready assets.

### Personality + Professionalism

The studio does not have to remove its personality to serve serious organizations.

Instead, the website should demonstrate that strong creative thinking and professional execution can exist together.

---

# 21. Project Success Criteria

The redesign should be considered successful when it accomplishes the following:

### Brand

- WestCose immediately feels distinct.
- The site accurately reflects the studio's personality.
- Visual quality matches or exceeds the quality of the work being sold.

### Portfolio

- Major work is easy to discover.
- Visitors understand the breadth of capabilities.
- High-value projects are presented as systems rather than image dumps.

### Business

- Visitors can quickly determine whether WestCose is a good fit.
- Qualified inquiries increase.
- Services are clearly differentiated.

### SEO

- Existing valuable rankings are preserved.
- Important current URLs are accounted for.
- New service pages expand organic search coverage.
- Case studies strengthen topical authority.

### Experience

- Motion feels cinematic rather than decorative.
- Desktop experience is memorable.
- Mobile experience remains intentional.
- Reduced-motion users retain the complete content experience.
- Performance remains acceptable despite advanced visuals.

### Ecosystem

- Designs, Labs, and Shop feel related.
- Their purposes are clearly differentiated.
- Visitors can move between them without confusing their roles.

---

# 22. Core Project Principle

The website should not attempt to convince people that WestCose is creative by saying it.

The experience itself should prove it.

At the same time, visual experimentation must serve a clear business purpose:

> **Show the work. Build trust. Establish value. Create a memorable experience. Generate the next project.**

---

# 23. Locked Foundation

For the current redesign, the following decisions should be treated as the working foundation:

- `westcosedesigns.com` remains the primary design-studio domain.
- Existing SEO authority should be preserved during migration.
- WestCose Designs becomes the dedicated visual-design portfolio and client lead platform.
- WestCose Labs remains the home for software, websites, SaaS, and digital products.
- WestCose Shop remains the home for WestCose apparel and merchandise.
- The Shop rebuild is a separate future project.
- The Labs build is a separate project.
- WestCose Designs will showcase brand identity, logo design, illustration, apparel design, brand development, corporate collateral, and visual systems.
- The new site will use a custom Next.js / React architecture.
- The homepage will follow the approved cinematic, scene-based storytelling direction.
- SEO, accessibility, performance, and lead generation remain first-class requirements alongside visual experimentation.
