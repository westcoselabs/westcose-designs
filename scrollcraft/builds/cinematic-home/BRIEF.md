# WestCose cinematic homepage

Approved direction: user-authored implementation plan, with preferences captured during planning. On implementation, the user explicitly deferred Higgsfield and requested the current video be retained.

## Creative authority and audience

- Vibe: tactile, cinematic, coastal print workshop. Preserve WestCose's orange, teal, bone, and graphite palette, hero4 artwork, and liquid-glass navigation.
- Journey: constructed WC opening; dimensional hero; four-work gallery; falling print centerpiece; six identity-system stages; sketchbook; corporate identity; three-world ecosystem; project inquiry.
- Energy: restrained opening, confident hero, quieter gallery, main peak in the falling-paper sequence, then increasingly deliberate presentations and a calm working form.
- Feeling: intrigue from the traced mark; confidence from the hero; curiosity from the gallery; immersion from large passing prints; clarity from the identity system; craft from curved sketchbook pages; trust from assembled stationery; possibility from the ecosystem; readiness at the brief.
- Signature: the final Vested sheet passes close to the visitor and becomes the brand-development board, without changing artwork or replacing the sheet with a second overlay.
- Aesthetic range: detailed illustrated brand, photographic environment, tactile paper. No generic counters, custom cursor, or extra spectacle around the form.
- Structure: the user selected connected chapters, not one continuous camera flight. Existing Next/GSAP/Lenis/R3F architecture is retained.
- Assets: actual logo SVG, portfolio/hero4.png, existing portfolio manifests and models; existing ten-second studio footage. No new AI assets generated in this implementation.

## Peak and authored stillness

It is the site where a falling print fills your view, then lands as a finished identity system.

The falling studio is the peak (440svh). The gallery immediately before it gives the artwork room to be inspected. Loader has a 350ms solid-logo hold. Identity stages and the final brief intentionally allow reading without idle camera movement.
